The bush food

A leaf with a long story

Lemon myrtle — Backhousia citriodora — is a native tree of the subtropical rainforests of Queensland and northern New South Wales, long known and used by Aboriginal peoples.

Its glossy leaves carry a bright, clean lemon fragrance. We pick it fresh and pair it with hand-squeezed lemons and local honey, in small batches, made with care.

Our story

Aboriginal owned. Community made.

Lemon Myrtle Sparkling was started by Aboriginal co-founder Stevie Thorpe and first launched for NAIDOC Week.

Stevie and Bijay Sigdel now run it together — a small business built on native ingredients, care in the making, and the simple joy of sharing a drink with community.
